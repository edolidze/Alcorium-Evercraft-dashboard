Methodology & Data Caveats

Source: Ver1.xlsx Sheet1, 417 rows (Jan–Oct 2026, 41 SKUs). Row 1 = "2026", row 2 = headers, data from row 3. Read with openpyxl data_only=True.

Availability: 96 rows with no promo dates = not yet launched at Alcorium. These are excluded from all models and treated as "not available", not as zero-discount observations.

Normalisation: Units per day = units ÷ window days (end − start + 1). For months with 2–3 price windows (split rows), window days are summed and discount is day-weighted. Motivation deduplicated across split rows of the same SKU-month (non-zero unique value used).

Launch months flagged: An Ambavi (4 SKUs, started 16 Jul), Ardfort Extra Sherry (started 16 Sep), Halstons motivation (started 15 Apr). Launch months excluded from elasticity and trend models; flagged elsewhere.

Elasticity method: Within-SKU demeaned log-log OLS on ln(units/day) ~ ln(discount fraction). Requires ≥ 3 non-launch observations per SKU. Pooled: β = 1.096, R² = 0.175, n = 302. Caution: summer brings both naturally higher sales and deeper discounts, potentially upward-biasing the elasticity estimate.

Motivation: Jan–Aug only (n = 198 within-SKU pairs). Sep–Oct excluded due to target-based scheme change. Unit unconfirmed. Monthly avg 3.8–4.3 leaves very little within-SKU variation for estimation.

Revenue & prices: All figures VAT-inclusive GEL. The regular price rose in Jul for Ambavi and Gogla's SKUs; row-level prices are used throughout.
